  1. 1

    You give the location

    With the address, the cadastral reference or a click on the map. We find the exact plot with the Cadastre's official geometry, never the one next door.

  2. 2

    We cross-check official sources

    Cadastre, current planning and that town's bylaws, article by article. Depending on what it is, a plot, a flat or a warehouse, we look up what applies.

  3. 3

    You get your video report

    What is allowed there, in an easy 3D video: parameters, permitted uses, buildable geometry and number of homes.
