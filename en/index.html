How it works

Four steps, no paperwork

  1. 1

    You mark a point

    On the map, or give us the address or the cadastral reference.

  2. 2

    We find your plot

    With its official Cadastre boundaries: the exact plot, never the one next door.

  3. 3

    We apply the planning rules

    The local plan in force at that location, article by article.

  4. 4

    We show it to you in 3D

    A 3D video and a PDF with the data, their sources and clear conclusions.
