What data we process

The data you give us in the form: name, email, phone, Instagram username if you choose to receive it there, the location you mark, the language of your summary and your comments.

What for

  • To prepare and send you the summary for the location you request. Legal basis: your request.
  • To contact you about that summary and, if you ask, about the report or study. Legal basis: your request.
  • To send you URBIABLE news and cases, only if you tick the box. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

How long

For as long as our relationship lasts and, afterwards, for as long as needed to meet legal obligations. Data for news, until you unsubscribe.

Who else has access

We do not share your data with third parties. To prepare and send your video report we use providers that process it on our behalf, under a data processing agreement:

  • Microsoft Azure: the service that receives your request, prepares the video and stores the files, in Spain (Spain Central region).
  • Neon: the request database, in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Brevo: email delivery, in the European Union.
  • GitHub: website hosting.
  • PostHog: website usage measurement, in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • FormSubmit: only as a fallback, if our service does not respond, so that your request is not lost.

Some of these providers may access the data from outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by the GDPR. The link to view or download your video report expires after 7 days.

Your rights

You can access, rectify or erase your data, object to or restrict its processing and request its portability by writing to hola@urbiable.com. If you believe we are not handling it properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Usage measurement

To improve the website we measure how it is used: pages viewed, where you come from, how far you scroll, which parts you look at, how you use the map and at which step of the request you stop. We also record your path through the page anonymously, with everything you type hidden. We do not store cookies or anything in your browser, and we do not use it for advertising. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the service; you can object by writing to hola@urbiable.com or by enabling «Do Not Track» in your browser.

Cookies

This website does not use its own analytics or advertising cookies. The map uses external services (OpenStreetMap, Instituto Geográfico Nacional, Spanish Directorate General for Cadastre and CartoCiudad) that may log technical connection data in order to display the maps.
